How to read this

A clue, not a verdict.

Posts counts each entity at most once per post, reducing the effect of repetition and spam. Mentions shows every extracted occurrence.

Types come from Compromise’s local rule-based English tagger. “Things” are common noun phrases rather than named entities.

Cleaner signals require mentions across at least three posts. Function words, filler, link fragments, and common profanity are excluded.

Imperfect by design means names without enough context can be misclassified. Select any row to inspect the recent posts behind it.
