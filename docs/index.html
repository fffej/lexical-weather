    How to read this

    A signal, not a verdict.

    Live rate is occurrences per million word tokens in the rolling Bluesky sample. Reposts are not feed-post records and are not counted.

    Difference is shown in bits: +1 bit means twice the historical rate, +2 means four times. “New” means absent from the 50,000-word reference vocabulary—not newly invented.

    Ranking combines the selected period’s frequency lift with the square root of live mentions. Repetition builds confidence without letting raw popularity entirely dominate.

    Historical reference averages normalized Google Books frequencies from the five decades in each period, distributed through Stanford’s HistWords project.

    Built on the public Bluesky Jetstream. Historical data from HistWords under PDDL 1.0.

    Book English and social English are different habitats. Language labels are author-supplied, tokenization is deliberately simple, and closing this tab clears the sample.
