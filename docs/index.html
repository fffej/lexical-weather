Method

How topics emerge.

Momentum compares short- and long-window topic rates. Newly corroborated clusters also receive temporary early-life momentum while a longer baseline forms.

Lexical gate tracks phrase and word-pair rates across independent authors. Replies, hashtags, non-English text, and exact repeats are excluded.

Hybrid grouping combines MiniLM sentence similarity with shared phrase evidence. Inference requests WebGPU when available and falls back to CPU if it cannot run.

Eligibility and score are separate. Three candidate posts from three recent sources are required first; qualified topics are then weighted 40% momentum, 20% candidate volume, 15% semantic coherence, 15% low accumulated history, and 10% recent-source spread.

Live posts come from the public Bluesky Jetstream. Batched candidate embeddings use all-MiniLM-L6-v2 through Transformers.js and WebGPU when available.

Language labels are author-supplied, semantic similarity is approximate, and all counters are bounded, decaying estimates rather than an archive.
