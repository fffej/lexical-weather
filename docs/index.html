How to read this

A clue, not a verdict.

Now and reference are percentages, so differently sized samples remain comparable. “Now” uses occurrences in the active dictionary.

Change shows how many times more common the word is now. “New” means it was absent from the reference, not newly invented. Select a word to inspect the posts behind it.

Cleaner signals require at least three occurrences. Function words, filler, link fragments, and common profanity are excluded.

The historical reference is one 1900–1999 average from Stanford HistWords’ Google Books English data, entirely pre-LLM.

Live posts from the public Bluesky Jetstream. Historical frequencies from Stanford HistWords under PDDL 1.0.

Book English and social English are different habitats. Language labels are author-supplied, and this dashboard describes a sample rather than the whole network.
